# Marketing surface — approval triage copy

Scope: the public control section (`#control`) and the “Know when to step in” benefit card. This is a copy-only change plus a generated stylesheet line for `.details-note`; no product behavior, tokens, screenshots of the dashboard, or claims ledger rows were otherwise changed.

## Why

The dashboard’s approval queue now orders pending requests by soonest deadline, then high risk and outward effect, with due-soon / high-risk / leaves-machine counts and filters. The public site previously described only a single approval (“allow it once or say no”), so the new triage behavior was not discoverable to a reader. The copy now states that behavior without claiming a measured speedup, a safety guarantee, or bulk/automatic approval.

## Copy

- Benefit card: “When several wait, the soonest deadline and risk come first.”
- Approval rules disclosure: “When several requests wait: Needs you orders them by soonest deadline, then high risk and outward effect. Counts and filters highlight due-soon, high-risk, and requests that leave the machine. Each decision stays one request at a time; there is no bulk approval.”
- No new hero, CTA, availability, provider, or security claim.

Claims authority: `docs/marketing-site.md` (“The approval section may say…”); product evidence: `docs/realignment/approval-triage-2026-10-22/`.
