# Cedar — Positioning Draft (Synthetic Fixture)

> **Status: synthetic fixture, not market research.** All product names, prices, and sources below are fabricated test data. Nothing here is a validated claim about a real product. Campaign work is a **draft for review only** and must not be published or treated as approved copy.

## Positioning recommendation

Position Cedar as the **review-first local document assistant**: it drafts document briefs and keeps state locally, and it is explicitly built so that a human reviews output before anything is shared.

Recommended frame: *"Draft locally. Review before you share."*

Why this frame:
- It rests only on the verified features given (drafts briefs; requires review before sharing; local state).
- It avoids competing head-on on price or hosting model, where the only inputs are two competitor price points ($12/month browser-only, $20/month hosted) with no feature parity data.
- The mandatory review step is framed as a deliberate control, not a shortcoming — but this is a **positioning hypothesis**, not a proven preference. It needs validation with real users before adoption.

## What we cannot claim

Do **not** put any of the following in copy — there is no supporting evidence:
- SOC 2 or any other certification, audit, or compliance status (for Cedar or for Birch/Elm).
- Conversion, retention, or performance/benchmark comparisons of any kind.
- "Cheaper than X" or "better than Y" framing beyond restating the listed prices.
- Security, privacy, or data-residency guarantees beyond the bare fact that state is local.

Prices ($12 and $20 per month) are recorded fixtures only; they are not verified current pricing and must be re-checked before any comparative use.

## Campaign ideas (drafts for review)

### 1. "Review Before Send" — workflow-led
- **Concept:** Show the draft → human review → share loop as the product's core motion, aimed at people who already have a review step in their process.
- **Format:** Short demo sequence plus a one-page workflow diagram.
- **Draft hook:** "The draft is the easy part. Here's where you check it."
- **Draft CTA:** "See the review step" (no availability, pricing, or trial claims until confirmed).
- **Needs before approval:** confirmation that the demo reflects actual shipping behaviour; a named owner for review copy.

### 2. "Local State, Local Drafts" — control-led
- **Concept:** Lead with documents and state staying on the machine, targeted at teams who care where working files live.
- **Format:** Explainer post plus FAQ.
- **Draft hook:** "Your working state stays where you work."
- **Draft CTA:** "Read how local state works" (avoid any security or compliance implication).
- **Needs before approval:** a factual, non-certification description of what "local state" does and does not cover; legal/compliance review given the risk of implied guarantees.

Both ideas are intentionally capability-descriptive rather than comparative. If a comparative angle is wanted later, it needs verified competitor feature data and current pricing — neither exists in this fixture.
