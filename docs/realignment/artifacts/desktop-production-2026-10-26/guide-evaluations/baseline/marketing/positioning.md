## Positioning recommendation

Cedar is the document assistant for people who want a first draft, not a final answer. Where Birch is browser-only and Elm is hosted, Cedar's verified differentiator is the combination of **local state** with a **mandatory review step before sharing**. Frame Cedar as the "draft, review, then send" assistant: it produces document briefs quickly, but a person always reviews the work before it goes anywhere.

Lead with the workflow, not with comparisons. Price is the honest wedge, not security: Cedar is positioned against Birch ($12/month) and Elm ($20/month) without claiming an advantage we cannot evidence.

**Positioning statement:** For teams that need document briefs drafted fast but must approve them before sharing, Cedar drafts the brief and keeps the workflow local and reviewable — a draft you sign off on, not an auto-send.

## Campaign idea 1 — "Draft, Review, Send"

A three-step explainer series that walks through the actual Cedar workflow. Each piece is one step: drafting a document brief, the review gate before sharing, and the local-state working model. Aimed at readers who are wary of tools that publish on their behalf.

- **Format:** three short posts or a single landing page in three panels.
- **Hook:** "The review step isn't a setting. It's the product."
- **Call to action:** walk through the workflow; no pricing or comparison claims in this campaign.
- **Why it fits:** it demonstrates the verified feature set instead of asserting superiority.
