## Positioning recommendation (draft)
Position Cedar as a **review-first document drafting assistant**: it produces brief drafts, and the
workflow is explicitly designed so a human reviews the draft before it is shared. Lead with the
review step as the product's operating principle rather than with unverified security, cost, or
speed claims.

Recommended core message (draft):
> Cedar drafts document briefs — and puts a human review step in front of anything you share.

Supporting proof points available today: (1) draft generation for document briefs; (2) mandatory
review before sharing; (3) local state as observed in the UI. Anything beyond these three points
needs independent verification before use.

Avoid in all Cedar copy until verified: SOC 2 or other certifications, "offline," "your data never
leaves the device," residency guarantees, percentage savings or price comparisons against Birch/Elm,
conversion or efficiency lift, and any "better than Birch/Elm" comparison.
