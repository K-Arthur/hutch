## Positioning recommendation

**Recommended position (draft):** Position Cedar as a *drafting-and-review* document assistant for people who want a brief drafted first and a human decision before anything is shared.

- **Category:** local document assistant (as stated).
- **Primary value proposition:** "Draft the brief, then review before you share." This rests only on the two verified Cedar features in the fixture.
- **Differentiator to test, not assert:** the review-before-sharing step versus a browser-only tool (Birch) and a hosted tool (Elm). The differentiator is a *workflow* difference that is directly stated, not a security, cost, or performance claim.
- **Audience hypothesis:** teams or individuals who circulate document briefs and need a check step before distribution. Unverified; requires ICP evidence.
