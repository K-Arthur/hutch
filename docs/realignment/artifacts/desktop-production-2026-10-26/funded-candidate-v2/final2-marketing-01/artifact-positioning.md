## 1. Positioning recommendation

Position Cedar as a **review-gated document briefing assistant**: it drafts document briefs,
and a human must review before anything is shared.

- **What we can say (supported by the fixture):** Cedar drafts document briefs; it requires
  review before sharing; its state is local.
- **What we cannot say:** no certification (e.g. SOC 2) is claimed for any product; no
  conversion, accuracy, speed, or cost-saving claim is supported; "local state" is not proof
  of local inference, offline operation, data residency, or absence of egress.
- **Price:** Cedar's price is not given in the source. Do not imply savings or cost advantage
  against Birch ($12/month) or Elm ($20/month); those are the only prices we have, and they are
  competitors' prices, not a comparison basis.

**Suggested headline direction (draft):** "Draft the brief. Review it. Then share." — a
process claim only, traceable to the stated features.

**Suggested CTA direction (draft):** "Request a review copy" / "See a draft" — an
evaluation-intent CTA. Avoid any CTA implying a certified, benchmarked, or cheaper product.

## 2. Campaign ideas

### Campaign A — "Review gate" (process-led, trust framing)
- **Angle:** the review step is the feature. Every brief is human-approved before it leaves
  the workspace.
- **Audience:** teams that share documents externally and want a human checkpoint.
- **Draft assets:** one landing headline (above), one short email, one demo script showing a
  draft → review → share sequence.
- **Evidence status:** fully supported by the fixture's stated features. Do not add security,
  compliance, or accuracy claims to strengthen it.
- **Open question:** what exactly does "local state" mean in the product? Unknown from source.

### Campaign B — "Compare before you commit" (evaluation-led)
- **Angle:** invite evaluation against browser-only (Birch, $12/month) and hosted (Elm,
  $20/month) alternatives, without asserting a winner.
- **Audience:** buyers currently comparing tool categories and deployment models.
- **Draft assets:** a comparison *template* with blank cells for price, deployment, and review
  workflow, plus a note to verify each vendor's claims directly.
- **Evidence status:** competitor category and price come from the fixture sources. Cedar's own
  price and deployment model are unknown, so no cell may be filled in for Cedar.
- **Risk:** any filled-in "better/cheaper" cell would be an unsupported claim. Keep the
  template blank.
