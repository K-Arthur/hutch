Opening your workspace

Local-first multi-agent platform

Hutch is starting on this computer. Your workspace will open here when it is ready.

Startup troubleshooting

  • Ensure no other local Hutch server process is occupying the backend port.
  • Check that your local Python 3.11+ runtime or virtual environment is intact.
  • Inspect ~/.local/share/hutch or terminal stderr for runtime logs.

Choose Quit Hutch from the system tray menu and reopen to retry.

Closing this window keeps Hutch in the system tray. Choose Quit Hutch from its menu to stop its work.
