Why this is the differentiator, precisely: the cap is a separate, wider horizontal mass with a visible underside line at the eaves. A mask, ghost, tombstone or app-tile silhouette has no projecting cap; the cap also stops the "floating head" read because it grounds the front as a built structure. It survives 16px as a 1.7px roof band above the mass, which is the first thing the eye resolves at tab size.

Colour: single-colour mark. The residents are true holes, so background shows through and no second colour is needed; emerald stays reserved for the wordmark period and the UI accent.
