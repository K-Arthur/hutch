D2 Canopy. A trapezoid cap (top 7..25 @ y1.4, eaves 1.5..30.5 @ y4.6, h3.2, overhang 2.5 beyond the arch) sits over the liked R7-4 front. The arch keeps its r12 crown, which tucks 0.6 under the cap; the two residents stay r3.4 knocked out at (11.5,17.5) and (20.5,17.5), 2.2 apart, 1.5 above the cap line and 7.1 above the floor. The cap is the hutch roof: it reads as a structure with a roof rather than a face, and it is the one part a stock mask/ghost silhouette cannot copy.

Small master (16-24px): cap 5..27 @ y0.8, eaves 1..31 @ y4.4; arch widened to 3..29 with r13 crown; residents r3.8 at 11.2/17.4 and 20.8/17.4 (2.0 apart, 4.4 to the sides). The cap is deliberately wider at small size so the roof line survives the 16px render.

Colour: single-colour mark; residents are real holes, so the background always shows through and no second colour is required. Emerald is reserved for the wordmark period and UI accent.
