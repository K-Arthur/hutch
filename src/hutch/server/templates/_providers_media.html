Search

provider search and Hutch's live_search are different tools

Provider search: the provider runs the search and writes the answer, so your query goes to that provider. live_search: Hutch itself fetches public pages through its approved egress and returns a brief with numbered citations. Neither replaces the other, and Hutch never switches from one to the other for you.
